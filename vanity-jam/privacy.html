Privacy Policy

Vanity Jam, last updated October 18, 2026

Vanity Jam is a puzzle game made by Kettlekite, the studio name of independent developer Mert Can Gözükara. We built it so that it works without knowing who you are.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. The game does not ask for an account, name, email address, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information, and it contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

What stays on your device

Your progress (current level, stars, gems, boosters and settings) is saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, the game schedules a notification for your next daily gift on your device, at the hour you usually play. That hour is worked out on your device from your own play times and is never sent anywhere. You can turn reminders off in the game's Settings or in iOS Settings.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Gems and boosters you buy are saved with your progress on your device.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example optional ads), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the App Store privacy details will change accordingly.

Contact

Questions? Write to support@kettlekite.com.
