Streetball

Fast three-a-side football in a caged court at sunset. Walls stay in play, passes are quick and every tackle counts, against the computer or friends online.

Free on Mac and Windows, and playable right in the browser. In beta.

Play in the browser Download on itch.io

LavaSpin

Party sumo on a magma orb. Two to four players shove, dash and slide each other into the lava, while the island shrinks under their feet.

Free on Mac and Windows, and in the browser. One keyboard, gamepads, or online with a room code.

Play in the browser Download on itch.io

Vanity Jam

The messiest makeup drawer, tidied one tap at a time. Every lipstick, brush and palette flies to its box, and anything lying on top has to go first.

A calm 3D puzzle for iPhone, two hundred levels, every one solvable. Coming soon to the App Store.

Visit Vanity Jam

Sundae Jam

A dessert counter jammed with scoops, wafers and waffles. Tap one with a clear way out and it slides into the glass that wants its flavour.

A 3D puzzle for iPhone with frozen desserts, padlocks and turntables, two hundred levels in all. Coming soon to the App Store.

Visit Sundae Jam

One person, every part of the game.

Kettlekite is the studio of Mert Can Gözükara, an independent developer in Leipzig. Each game is designed, coded, modelled, lit and shipped by the same pair of hands, from the first sketch to the store page.

The desktop games are free on itch.io and play in the browser. The iPhone games come with no account, no ads and no tracking.
